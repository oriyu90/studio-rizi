2026.09.04UPDATE

Fcam pro v1.0.2 is available.

Adds a capture-panel button to open the last photo or video in the system gallery, tap to lock / unlock focus, a per-control and “all” return-to-Auto for manual settings, a rule-of-thirds grid, and a lens-switch pop-up in photo and video mode. It also reworks start-up stalls, preview interruption on rotation, resource release when switching photo↔video, the pinch-zoom limit, self-timer cancellation, dropped capture requests from other apps, and background-recording reliability.

2026.09.03UPDATE

WAKARU v0.0.4 is available.

Scanned PDF pages with no text layer and images that contain text are now OCR’d and made searchable (a pure-Rust engine; models fetched once). A scanned PDF also gets a searchable copy with an invisible, position-matched text layer. Studio gains a build_document tool that turns a title and sections into a formatted Markdown, Word or PDF document. Distribution is currently invite-only.
