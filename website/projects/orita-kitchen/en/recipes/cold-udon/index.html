02 / HOW TO COOK

Method

  1. 01

    Bring water to a boil

    Fill a small saucepan about 80% full with water. Bring to a boil over high heat.

  2. 02

    Add the udon

    Add one portion of frozen udon. Leave it until the water is about to return to a boil.

  3. 03

    Loosen and drain

    Just before boiling, stir gently with chopsticks. As soon as it boils, drain through a colander.

  4. 04

    Cool under running water

    Run cold water through the colander. Gently rub the noodles to remove surface starch and heat, for about 20 seconds.

  5. 05

    Serve

    Put the noodles in a bowl with your chosen sauce and prepared toppings.

Also check the heating instructions on your frozen udon package.

03 / TOPPINGS

Preparing the toppings

Wakame

Place the wakame in a measuring cup with four times its volume of water. Microwave for 10 seconds. Check hydration and leave in water longer if needed.

Scallions

Use frozen chopped scallions as they are. If uncut, simply wash and chop.

Aburaage

Use the sliced fried tofu as it is.
