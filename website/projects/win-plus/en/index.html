FIVE JOBS, ONE TRAY ICON

At your fingertips.

Everything is GUI-configurable. After launch it lives in the tray, idling at 0% CPU and about 50 MB.

01

Touchpad mapping

Three-finger tap for Task View, three-finger scroll for virtual desktops, and more. Includes a finger-count meter.

↘
02

Device recognition

Lists connected devices by real name and bus (USB/Bluetooth/internal). Per-device key rules included.

↘
03

Custom shortcuts

32 presets (media keys, volume, Win+D…) plus free input. Click a field and press the keys to register.

↘
04

Screenshots

Multiple profiles, full/window/region capture, per-profile folders, drag & drop from the preview. QR links in frame are listed for one-tap opening.

↘
05

Quick memo

A tiny popup on one hotkey. Auto-closes in 6 seconds, survives reboots. Paste becomes plain text; images only.

↘

THE REAL APPLICATION

The settings, as they are.

Attached real screen. Left nav plus settings cards operate every feature from the GUI. 7-language UI.

About dev signing

The current release is developer self-signed. If SmartScreen warns, continue from details. A CA-signed build is planned. Settings and memos live in OS-standard locations and are removed on uninstall.
