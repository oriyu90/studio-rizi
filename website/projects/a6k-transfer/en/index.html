Straight from your camera
to your phone, over Wi-Fi

α6k Transfer connects directly to the Wi-Fi of Sony α6000-series cameras and transfers your photos and videos to your phone. No cables, no cloud. Set the camera to “Select on Smartphone” → “Displaying”, pick items from the list, and save them to your gallery. Transfers continue in the background.

Features

Direct Wi-Fi transfer

Join the camera access point (DIRECT-…) from inside the app, browse photos and videos over DLNA/UPnP, and save only the items you pick at full size. Transfers keep going in the background.

Already-saved badges

Transferred files stay marked “Saved” via on-device history, so you never transfer the same file twice. Filter by date and media type (photos/videos) and set a custom save folder.

Language & theme switch

Switch the UI between Japanese, English, and system language, and the theme between light, dark, and system — instantly, from in-app settings.

Camera profiles

The α6000 is verified on-device. The α6100 and later are covered by a predictive, unverified profile so future models fit the same design.

How to use

  1. On the camera: MENU → Send to Smartphone → Select on Smartphone → “Displaying”
  2. In the app’s Connect tab: “Connect + bind” (a system approval dialog appears on first use)
  3. “Discover camera” to lock the LOCATION, then load the list in the Transfer tab, select, and transfer
