From camera to pocket, over Wi-Fi

α6k Transfer joins the Wi-Fi of Sony α6000-series cameras directly and moves your photos and videos to your phone. No cables, no cloud. Set the camera to “Select on Smartphone” → “Displaying”, pick items from the list, and save them to your gallery. Transfers continue in the background.

What it does

List, move, keep. The round trip with the camera folds into three moves.

Direct Wi-Fi

No cable, no cloud

Join the camera access point (DIRECT-…) from inside the app, browse photos and videos over DLNA/UPnP, and save only the items you pick at full size. Transfers keep going in the background.

DIRECT-cNE0:a6000 · 10.0.0.1

History

Already-saved badges

Transferred files stay marked “Saved” via on-device history, so you never transfer the same file twice. Filter by date and media type, and set a custom save folder.

Background

Runs while you leave

A foreground service carries progress with a notification. Screen off, app switched away — the transfer does not stop. Cancel any time.

Media

Stills and motion, together

Pick JPEG photos and MP4 videos apart in the list. RAW (.ARW and friends) is not exposed by the α6000 over DLNA, so it stays out of scope.

Language

Tongue and theme switch

Switch the UI between Japanese, English, and system language, and the theme between light, dark, and system — instantly, from in-app settings.

Profile

Camera profiles

The α6000 is verified on-device. The α6100 and later are covered by a predictive, unverified profile so future models fit the same design.

How to use

One move on the camera, two in the app.

  1. 01

    Set the camera to “Displaying”

    MENU → Send to Smartphone → Select on Smartphone → “Displaying”. The camera opens its access point.

  2. 02

    Connect and bind

    In the app’s Connect tab: “Connect + bind”. A system approval dialog appears on first use.

  3. 03

    Discover, then transfer

    “Discover camera” locks the LOCATION, then load the list in the Transfer tab, select, and transfer. Files land in your gallery.
